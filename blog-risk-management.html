Why Risk Management Comes First

Understand risk planning, stop-loss concepts, and why trading outcomes are never guaranteed.

Risk management is the process of deciding how much uncertainty and potential loss you are willing to accept before entering a trade. It cannot eliminate risk, but it can help make decisions more deliberate.

Define risk before entry

A trading plan may include an invalidation level, stop-loss approach, position size, and maximum risk limits. A stop-loss order does not guarantee execution at the requested price during fast or illiquid markets.

Understand leverage

Leverage can magnify both gains and losses. A small market move may have a large effect on account equity, and losses can exceed expectations.

Review the process

Keeping a trading journal can help identify whether decisions followed the plan. No risk framework guarantees profits or prevents losses.
