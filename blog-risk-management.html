RISK MANAGEMENT

Why Risk Management Comes First

A practical introduction to thinking about risk before a trade.

Learning note: This article is for educational purposes only and is not financial advice.

Risk is part of every trade

Markets are uncertain. Even a well-researched idea can move against a trader, and leverage can magnify losses. Risk management is about understanding potential downside—not eliminating it.

Know your exposure

Position size, leverage, stop placement and correlated positions all affect exposure. Learn how these elements interact before using a live account. A stop order may not always execute at the expected price, especially in fast-moving or gapping markets.

Use a written plan

A trading plan can define the conditions you are studying, what would invalidate an idea and how you will review the result. A plan supports consistency but cannot guarantee an outcome.

Review process, not just profit

Record whether you followed your plan, what assumptions you made and what you learned. A profitable result does not necessarily mean a sound decision, and a losing result does not automatically mean the process was poor.
