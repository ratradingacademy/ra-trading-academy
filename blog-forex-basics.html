FOREX BASICS

What Is Forex Trading?

A beginner-friendly introduction to the foreign exchange market.

Learning note: This article is for educational purposes only and is not financial advice.

The foreign exchange market

Forex, or foreign exchange, is a global market where currencies are exchanged. Participants include banks, businesses, institutions, governments and individual traders. Currency prices move in response to many factors, including economic data, interest rates and market expectations.

How currency pairs work

Forex is commonly quoted in pairs, such as EUR/USD. The first currency is the base currency and the second is the quote currency. A quoted price expresses how much of the quote currency is needed for one unit of the base currency.

What beginners should understand

Learn the meaning of pips, spreads, leverage and margin before considering any trade. Leverage can magnify both gains and losses. Beginners can use educational examples and demo environments to become familiar with order types and price movement.

Start with education and risk awareness

No single method guarantees trading success. Before risking money, learn how losses can occur, consider whether trading is appropriate for your circumstances and never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

Forex trading involves risk, including the possible loss of capital. This content does not recommend any particular trade, broker or strategy.
